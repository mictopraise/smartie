# Two-minute demo plan (record after real profiling)

This is a recording plan, not final Devpost copy.

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

Show an ordinary laptop with networking disabled. In one sentence: many African small businesses work through messaging on unreliable connectivity and cannot depend on a paid cloud assistant.

## 0:20–0:40 — Engineering choice

Show the repository and model filename. State: Qwen2.5 1.5B Instruct, Q4_K_M GGUF, approximately 1.12 GB, CPU-only through llama.cpp, with no network during inference.

## 0:40–1:20 — Live proof

Run one payment-follow-up example, then the laundry-business arithmetic prompt. Keep the terminal command and complete output visible. Do not cut away during generation unless the final video would exceed two minutes.

## 1:20–1:45 — Measured evidence

Show the real `submission.json` and highlight generation tokens/second, peak RSS, and thermal status. Say the exact laptop specification. Do not use estimates or another machine's figures.

## 1:45–2:00 — Close

Summarize: private, offline, practical business outputs on hardware people already own. End with the public repository URL and team name.

## Capture checklist

- Airplane mode or disconnected network is visibly demonstrated.
- No personal customer data appears on screen.
- Terminal font is readable at 1080p.
- Every spoken benchmark number matches `submission.json`.
- Video duration is at most 2:00.
- Add captions and a repository link in the description.
